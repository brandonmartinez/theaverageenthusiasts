If you subscribed years ago

Do nothing. It still works.

The show’s original feed lived at assets.theaverageenthusiasts.com/podcast/feed.xml, and that address still answers. It now also carries the standard “this feed has moved” marker (<itunes:new-feed-url>), so Apple Podcasts and most other apps will quietly repoint themselves here the next time they refresh. Nothing breaks in the meantime, and nothing needs to be re-added.

The episode audio has not moved at all. It is served from the same place it has been served from since 2017, so every old link, bookmark, and cached enclosure keeps resolving.

The reason for the move is unglamorous: a domain and a feed should outlive whatever infrastructure happened to be underneath them at the time.
